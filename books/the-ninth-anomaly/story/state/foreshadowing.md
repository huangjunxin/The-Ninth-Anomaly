## Memory burn-list seeding rule (critical)
Chapter 14 burns Arthur's memories one by one. Chapters 1–8 MUST plant each item as concrete, specific scenes first, or the climax has nothing to spend. Plant list:
- The confiscated picture book: a specific page (e.g., a tabby on a windowsill, a caption line).
- His mother's voice: a specific phrase / song fragment.
- The temperature of one specific afternoon (the day of the raid).
- The word "cat" itself — the first word he learned to hide.
- Small extras welcome (a rain smell he once smelled pre-Culling, the weight of a blanket), keep them consistent.
